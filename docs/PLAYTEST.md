# Playtest log

## Playtest checklist (do this before judging)

1. Give it to 3 to 5 kids aged 7 to 12 with no instructions. Do they tap the outpost without reading?
2. Note where they get stuck in the first two minutes.
3. Do they understand why the cellar matters? Ask them.
4. Check phone and laptop layouts, keyboard-only play, and sound on and off.
