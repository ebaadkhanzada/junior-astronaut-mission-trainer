How to play

  • WalkClick or tap the ground to walk there. Or use WASD or the arrow keys.
  • WorkClick a glowing machine to walk to it and use it. Or stand close and press E. Clean dust, grab rocks, run experiments, grow food.
  • Power is tightSolar panels only work in daylight. The scrubber, grow lights, radio and lab all use battery. Turn things off to save it.
  • Storms are realEach mission replays real solar storms from NASA’s records. Keep the radio on to hear warnings, then run to the Storm Cellar.
